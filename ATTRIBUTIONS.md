Commons during the October 2026 refinement pass, downloaded and stored locally
in `public/refs/`. License metadata was read from each file's Commons page at
download time; each board tile also carries a visible credit line.

Everything else in the project (diagrams, textures, UI studies, the three
archival figures on /landmarks) is original CSS/SVG work.
