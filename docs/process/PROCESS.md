# Process record

The honest history of how this project changed, iteration by iteration. The
hub's "Process notes" section is the short version; this file is the record.
Screenshots here are curated from the verification runs (full sets are
regenerated by `scripts/verify/`, which gitignores its output).

## Iterations

1. **Initial build (Oct 13, daytime).** Scaffold → hub → three mood boards →
   three design systems → the Editorial site, committed in stages. A
   Playwright browser review before first deployment caught
   background-colored swatches with no border and three low-contrast labels;
   fixed, then deployed.
2. **Written design review → deep revision (Oct 13, later).** A detailed
   review brief (accepted by the author) drove: a primary-source content
   audit (see ../../SOURCES.md), the crimson-as-action-only accent policy,
   complete control-state matrices and working demos in all three systems, a
   native-dialog panel with focus trap/restoration, radiogroup filters,
   archival SVG figures, and honest hub process notes.
3. **Author review → visual refinement (this iteration).** The author
   reviewed the deployed site and asked for stronger, more distinctive
   visuals, agreeing with a second review's goals. This pass: fourteen
   sourced archival references on the mood boards (licenses recorded in
   ../../ATTRIBUTIONS.md), anchor-scale board compositions, a hub rebuilt
   around real comparable previews, a beginner-friendly copy pass, the
   unified FIG. plate series with drop-cap story openings, continuous-reading
   polish (next/previous starts at the top with focus on the new heading),
   section indexes and demo fixes in the design systems, and this durable
   docs/ + scripts/ evidence.

## Who did what

The author chose the topic, the three styles, the Editorial direction, and
(in iteration 3) personally reviewed the site and set the "stronger, cooler"
goal. The written review briefs set the revision goals. The agent chose the
specific design solutions and implementations under those directions. All
"verified" claims refer to agent-driven Playwright checks — not human
testing. Classmate sessions (docs/REVIEW_EXERCISE.md) had not yet been run
when this was written.
