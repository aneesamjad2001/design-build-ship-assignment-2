# Classmate review exercise

A 10-minute usability pass for two or three classmates, run by the author.
Give the reviewer the live link and read the tasks aloud one at a time. Watch
silently; note where they hesitate. Do not help until a task is clearly stuck.

**Site:** https://design-build-ship-assignment-2-sigma.vercel.app

## Tasks

1. **Find a breakthrough related to games.** (Expected: the Games filter or
   scanning to Deep Blue / AlphaGo.)
2. **Open an entry you've never heard of and explain it back in your own
   words.** (Tests the no-jargon-assumed writing.)
3. **Mark an entry as read, then undo it.** (Tests the primary action and the
   reversible state.)
4. **Find the original source for that entry.** (Expected: the Sources list
   in the story panel.)
5. **Looking at the hub, which visual direction was chosen for the site, and
   why do you think it fits?** (Tests the hub's selected-direction story.)
